Part 1

What Is an Algebraic Expression?

An algebraic expression uses numbers, variables, and operations (+ − × ÷). It has no equal sign.

3n + 5
Example: A bottle of soft drink costs ₱20. For n bottles, the cost is 20n pesos.
Remember: An expression is a phrase — it does not have a complete sentence or equal sign.
Part 2

Parts of an Expression

Variable
A letter for an unknown number: n
Coefficient
The number multiplying a variable: 3
Constant
A fixed number: 5
Term
Parts separated by + or −: 3n and 5
Example: In 5x − 2
Variable = x  |  Coefficient = 5  |  Constant = −2
Part 3

Translating Words to Algebra

Look for key words that tell you which operation to use.

➕ Addition
sum, plus, more than, increased by, added to
➖ Subtraction
difference, minus, less than, decreased by, subtracted from
✖️ Multiplication
product, times, twice (2×), triple (3×), of, per
➗ Division
quotient, divided by, ratio, shared equally
Watch out! "5 less than n" means n − 5, NOT 5 − n. "Less than" and "subtracted from" reverse the order.
Twice a number
2n
Half a number
n ÷ 2  or  n/2
Sum of two numbers
x + y
Difference of two numbers
x − y
Part 4

Real-Life Examples 🇵🇭

1. Sari-sari store. Pandesal costs ₱3 each. Ana buys p pieces → 3p. For 10 pieces: 3(10) = ₱30.
2. Market. Rice is ₱56 per kilo and eggs are ₱10 each. Buying r kilos of rice and e eggs → 56r + 10e. For 2 kg, 6 eggs: 56(2) + 10(6) = 112 + 60 = ₱172.
3. Change. You pay ₱500 for goods worth x pesos → change = 500 − x. If x = 342, change = ₱158.
4. Savings. You have ₱500 and save n pesos weekly → total = 500 + n. After 10 weeks saving ₱100: 500 + 100(10) = ₱1,500.
Steps: Read → Define variable → Write expression → Substitute values → Calculate → Check units (₱, kg, km).
Part 5

Formulas and Evaluating

A formula is an equation showing a relationship between quantities. To evaluate, replace each variable with its value, then compute step by step.

Perimeter of Rectangle
P = 2l + 2w
Simple Interest
I = Prt
Jeepney Fare
F = 13 + 2d
Area of Rectangle
A = l × w
Example: Fencing a lot. l = 12 m, w = 8 m
P = 2(12) + 2(8) = 24 + 16 = 40 m
Example: Savings interest. P = ₱5,000, r = 0.02, t = 3 years
I = 5000 × 0.02 × 3 = ₱300
Example: Fare. d = 5 km extra
F = 13 + 2(5) = 13 + 10 = ₱23
Tip: Always multiply before adding. Use parentheses when substituting: 2(12) not 212.
