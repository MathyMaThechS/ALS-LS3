Properties of Real Numbers & Number Lines

For Alternative Learning System Learners

1. What are Real Numbers?

Real numbers include all numbers you use in daily life — counting numbers, whole numbers, fractions, decimals, positive and negative numbers. They can be represented on a number line.

📌 Sets of Real Numbers

  • Natural Numbers (Counting Numbers): 1, 2, 3, 4, ...
  • Whole Numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, ...
  • Integers: ..., −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ... (no fractions or decimals)
  • Rational Numbers: Can be written as a fraction a/b (examples: ½, 0.75, −⅔)
  • Irrational Numbers: Cannot be written as simple fractions (examples: π, √2)

  • Fractions/decimals fall between the integer marks.

3. Properties of Real Numbers

🔄 Commutative Property

Changing order does not change the result.

Addition: a + b = b + a

Example: 4 + 2 = 2 + 4 → 6 = 6 ✔️

Multiplication: a × b = b × a

Example: 3 × 5 = 5 × 3 → 15 = 15 ✔️

⚠️ Does NOT work for subtraction or division!

🔗 Associative Property

Changing grouping (parentheses) does not change the result.

Addition: (a + b) + c = a + (b + c)

Example: (2 + 3) + 4 = 2 + (3 + 4) → 9 = 9 ✔️

Multiplication: (a × b) × c = a × (b × c)

Example: (2 × 3) × 4 = 2 × (3 × 4) → 24 = 24 ✔️

✨ Identity Property

Adding or multiplying by a special number leaves the original number the same.

Addition: a + 0 = a

Zero (0) is the additive identity → 7 + 0 = 7 ✔️

Multiplication: a × 1 = a

One (1) is the multiplicative identity → −5 × 1 = −5 ✔️

⚖️ Inverse Property

Combining a number with its inverse gives the identity value.

Addition: a + (−a) = 0

Opposite numbers sum to zero → 6 + (−6) = 0 ✔️

Multiplication: a × (1/a) = 1  (a ≠ 0)

Reciprocals multiply to one → 5 × (1/5) = 1 ✔️

⚡ Zero Product Property

If product of two numbers is zero, at least one of them must be zero.

If a × b = 0  then  a = 0  or  b = 0  (or both)

Example: (x − 2)(x + 3) = 0 → x = 2 or x = −3 ✔️

🔒 Closure Property

When you operate on two numbers from a set, the result stays in the same set.

  • Real numbers: Real ± Real = Real; Real × Real = Real ✔️
  • Integers: Integer ± Integer = Integer; Integer × Integer = Integer ✔️
  • Not closed under division: 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 (not an integer)
