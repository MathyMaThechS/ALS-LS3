🎯 About This ALS Learning Platform

I created this website to develop and share complete, interactive lessons specifically for ALS Learning Strand 3 — Mathematical and Problem-Solving Skills.


Many ALS learners need a strong, clear foundation in math to progress — whether for Accreditation & Equivalency (A&E), Senior High School, or daily life. These lessons are designed to be accessible, self-paced, and practical, with examples relevant to Filipino learners.


My goal: Make quality math education free and available anywhere, anytime — on your phone, no special software needed. Every lesson includes explanations, examples, and practice tests to help you track your progress.


About the Creator: I am a Licensed Professional Teacher with a passion for reaching every learner, especially those in Alternative Learning Systems. Technology makes it possible to bring the classroom to you.

📖 Lessons — In Sequence

Start from Lesson 1 and go in order for best results:

  • 01 Perfect Squares, Cubes & Radicals Square roots, cube roots, radical basics — 10-item Pre-Test & 20-item Post-Test
  • 02 Sets and Venn Diagrams Notation, union, intersection, complement, real-life word problems
  • 03.1 Multiplication & Division of Integers Sign rules, step-by-step examples, practice quizzes
  • 03.2 Addition & Subtraction of Integers Number line, sign combinations, practice with answer keys
  • 04 Properties of Real Numbers Commutative, Associative, Distributive, Identity, Inverse properties
  • 05 Laws of Exponents Product, quotient, power rules — 10-item Pre-Test & 20-item Post-Test
  • 06 Algebraic Expressions & Formulas Translate words to algebra, evaluate expressions, real-life applications
